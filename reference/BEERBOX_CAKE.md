# 🎂 BEER BOX CAKE — Tina's recipe (LOCKED — ready to WOW-author)
*Tina's own, always-baked birthday cake — baked in a **foil-lined beer box**, which is where the name comes from. A known SA tradition (baking a cake in a foil-lined beer box for a crowd); **this is Tina's own adapted, tested version** — author her amounts, original prose, never a copied recipe. Chocolate is the flagship 🏆; vanilla is the variation (drop the cacao, raise the flour). Three sizes: **½ · Normal · Extra full**.*

## 📦 How the sizes work (the vessel is the story)
- **½** → a *half-size* beer box, filled ~half its depth.
- **Normal** → a *normal* beer box, filled ~half its depth.
- **Extra full** → the Normal recipe **+ a half** (= 1.5×), which fills the *normal* box **to capacity** → a taller cake, **more people off one cake**.
- Insight for MF122: this cake grows by **filling the same box deeper**, not by a wider tin — a distinct scaling mode worth remembering.

## 📐 Units (locked)
Liquids in **ml**, dry in **grams**. Cacao kept in **ml** (Tina's tested measure). The extra-full "g" on oil/cacao was a slip — it's **ml** like the rest. All flour numbers are **Tina's tested amounts** (vanilla differs from choc because no cacao is added — not a scaling error, don't "correct" them).

## ① WET (bloomed together — cacao dissolves in the hot water)
| ingredient | ½ | Normal | Extra full |
|---|---|---|---|
| hot water | 250 ml | 500 ml | 750 ml |
| oil | 80 ml | 160 ml | 240 ml |
| **cacao** *(CHOCOLATE only — omit for vanilla)* | 80 ml | 160 ml | 240 ml |
| vanilla | 5 ml | 10 ml | 15 ml |

## ② EGGS + SUGAR
| ingredient | ½ | Normal | Extra full |
|---|---|---|---|
| eggs | 4 | 8 | 12 |
| sugar | 315 g | 630 g | 945 g |

## ③ DRY
| ingredient | ½ | Normal | Extra full |
|---|---|---|---|
| flour — **CHOCOLATE** | 230 g | 460 g | 670 g |
| flour — **VANILLA** | 270 g | 525 g | 800 g |
| baking powder | 5 g | 10 g | 15 g |
| salt | 2.5 ml | 5 ml | 7.5 ml |

---

## 👩‍🍳 CAKE METHOD
1. **Mix the dry** (③): flour, baking powder, salt.
2. **Beat the eggs, add the sugar**, and beat until pale and creamy (②).
3. **Add the dry (③) into the egg-sugar (②)** and mix in.
4. **Add the wet (①)** — hot water, oil, cacao (for chocolate) and vanilla. Blooming the cacao in the boiling water is what deepens the chocolate; the thin, hot batter is normal and bakes beautifully moist.
5. **Don't overmix** — overbeating knocks air in and causes bubbles.
6. **Bake at 180 °C** until a skewer comes out clean.
7. **The box:** line the beer box with foil — cover the whole box, underneath and on top — and bake the cake inside it.

## 🍫 ICING

### A · Classic boiled-water icing (Tina's — amounts for **Normal**; halve for ½, ×1.5 for Extra full)
- **Chocolate:** 800 g icing sugar · 100 g cacao · **5 ml vanilla essence** · boiling water
- **Vanilla:** 900 g icing sugar · 5 ml lemon juice · **5 ml vanilla essence** · boiling water
- *Vanilla essence goes in **both**. Scale: 2.5 ml (½) · 5 ml (Normal) · 7.5 ml (Extra full).*

**Method:**
1. **Sift** the icing sugar (and the cacao, for chocolate) through a sieve into a bowl — with a water icing there's no butter to smooth out lumps, so sifting is the whole trick to a glassy finish.
2. Stir in the **vanilla essence** (and, for vanilla, the **lemon juice** — it brightens the sweetness and helps the icing set).
3. **Add boiling water a tablespoon at a time**, stirring hard after each, until it falls from the spoon in a thick, glossy ribbon that spreads but doesn't run. Boiling water (not cold) dissolves the sugar to a smooth, shiny glaze that sets with a light crust.
4. Pour and spread over the **cooled** cake; leave to set.

### B · Buttercream variation ✅ (LOCKED — Normal amounts; halve for ½, ×1.5 for Extra full)
**250 g soft butter : 500 g icing sugar** (1 : 2), plus **5 ml vanilla essence** and a splash of milk. For **chocolate**, beat in **≈60 g cacao**. Cream the butter until pale, beat in the sifted icing sugar (and cacao), loosen with milk to a soft, spreadable buttercream. Richer and softer than the water icing.

### C · Condensed-milk caramel ✅ (our favourite — LOCKED)
Use **cooked (caramelised) condensed milk** — SA "Caramel Treat", or make your own by simmering a tin of condensed milk.
- **½:** ½ tin · **Normal:** 1½ tins (or 1 tin if spread thinly) · **Extra full:** 1½ tins (same as Normal)
Spread **as is** over the cooled cake. *(Optional: whip with a little soft butter for a fluffier caramel icing.)*

### Optional toppings
Sprinkles, chocolate chips, shaved chocolate — press them on **while the icing is still soft** so they stick.
