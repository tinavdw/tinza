# 🥫 MF184 — §38 DRAINED WEIGHTS · WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND
**Written 11 Aug 2026. Researched online, not off a shelf.**

> ⚖️ **NOTHING APPLIED TO `prices.js` IN THIS BRIEF.** Read §3 before applying anything —
> the research moved the blocker, it did not remove it.

---

## 1 · 🔴 THE HEADLINE — THE MISSING HALF SWAPPED OVER

§38.3 has said since 3 Aug that eleven keys are blocked on **a drained weight off the back of a tin.**

**That is no longer the binding constraint for most of them.** The drained figures are published,
and for the Koo range they are published by the retailer, not inferred. What is now missing on
almost every key is **the price of the exact tin the label belongs to** — because the prices
recorded in `prices.js` were taken at tin sizes that do not always match the labelled product.

⚖️ **A drained weight from a 410 g Koo tin divided into a price recorded for a 400 g tin is not
a correction. It is two products averaged into one wrong number.** That is the §38 Arm 3 failure
written out in full, and it is why nothing below has been applied.

⭐ **THE KOO 410 g NUMBER IS THE REAL FIND.** Three separate Koo 410 g bean lines — butter beans,
red kidney beans and bean mix — all print **drained mass 260 g**, i.e. **63 % of net**. That is a
product-line constant, published, and it covers most of the bean keys in one go.

---

## 3 · ⛔ WHY NOTHING WAS APPLIED

**One number closes all four bean keys at once: the shelf price of a Koo 410 g tin.**

With that, every bean key derives cleanly:

```
price of one 410 g Koo tin ÷ 0.260 kg = price per kg of actual bean
```

Worked as an illustration only, **not to be pasted**: at the R27 currently recorded against
`butter beans`, that is R27 ÷ 0.260 = **R104/kg**, against R68 live — a 53 % rise, and it would
move every recipe keying a tinned bean in the corpus. ⚖️ Far too large a move to make on a price
recorded for a differently-sized tin.

🩸 **AND THE SIZE MISMATCH IS NOT A ROUNDING.** `butter beans` records 400 g, `sugar beans` records
410 g, and `black beans` and `tinned cannellini beans` both record 400 g. Some of those are Koo
tins and some are own-brand, and the two are not the same product line or the same drained
fraction. Guessing which is which manufactures exactly the error §38 Arm 3 exists to prevent.

---

## 4 · ✅ WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT

⚖️ **This is now a two-column shopping note, not eleven photographs.**

For each tin actually bought: **the brand and size on the front, and the price paid.** The drained
weight for the Koo 410 g line is already known and does not need re-checking.

| still needs | keys it closes |
|---|---|
| price of one Koo 410 g bean tin | `butter beans` · `sugar beans` · `kidney beans` |
| brand, size and price of the black bean and cannellini tins bought | `black beans` · `tinned cannellini beans` |
| drained mass printed on the artichoke tin | `artichoke hearts` |
| confirmation the asparagus is a 330 g pack | `tinned asparagus` |

⛔ **`fruit cocktail` is the one that could move on today's evidence** — the tin size on record
(410 g) matches the composition data, giving ~275 g drained against R40, i.e. **~R145/kg** against
R98 live. ⚖️ It is still a derived fraction rather than a printed drained mass, so under Arm 4 it
would ship marked `est`, and an `est` key may never underwrite a cost claim (§37 interaction).
**Held pending a word, because it is a 48 % move.**

## 5 · 🔭 STILL UNRESOLVED, AND NOT A LABEL QUESTION

**Two bases live in `prices.js` at the same time, flagged in the file and never picked.**
Tinned beans are priced on **net tin weight** — butter beans R27/400 g, black beans R20/400 g,
baked beans R17/410 g — while the §38 keys are priced on **drained**. The file says outright that
this is a real inconsistency and that it was deliberately not resolved silently.

⚖️ Applying §38 to the bean keys **is** the resolution, and it is a corpus-wide cost move.
That is Tina's call, not a session's.
