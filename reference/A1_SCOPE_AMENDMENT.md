## ⚖️ **A1 AMENDED — A COUNTRY CLOSES WHEN ITS CANON IS COVERED, NOT WHEN IT REACHES 50**
**RULED 30 Jul 2026 (Tina)**

**The original scope.** China 50 · Japan 50 · Indonesia 50 · Thailand 50 · Vietnam 40 = **240**.

**Tina, 30 Jul 2026:** *"if there isnt proper recipes, rather we start with new country, 42 recipes

### THE RULING
1. ⚖️ **50 IS A TARGET, NOT A GATE.** A country closes when the dishes still available no longer
   clear the WOW bar — not when a counter reaches a round number. **Indonesia closes at 42.**
2. ⚖️ **THE REASON IS STRUCTURAL AND IT WILL RECUR, SO IT IS WORTH NAMING.** A3 requires exactly
   three versions per record. Across 42 records that is **126 versions**, and a large share of a
   country's canon ends up absorbed as **forks rather than records** — Indonesia's file carries
   Gudeg, Karedok, Nasi Kuning, Dawet Ayu, Serundeng Kelapa, Bebek Betutu, Sate Tempe, Rendang
   Tempe, Klepon Ubi and Pepes Tahu inside other cards. ✅ **The version rule is doing its job. The
   consequence is that a country is culturally complete well before it is numerically complete.**
3. 📌 **THE SYMPTOM TO WATCH FOR, because it is what gave it away.** Late-lane queue entries start
   dying on inspection rather than on price. Indonesia's last ten candidates included **six already
   in the file** — Kue Putu was Klepon · Serundeng was Urap-Urap's v3 · Gudeg was record 12 · Bebek
   Betutu was Ayam Betutu's v3 — plus two blocked on the egg ceiling and three blocked on absent
   prices. ⚖️ **When two consecutive queue entries are struck for being already-owned rather than
   unbuyable, the country is telling you it is finished.**
4. ✅ **A CLOSED COUNTRY CAN BE TOPPED UP LATER AND IS NOT SEALED.** Indonesia's genuinely-absent,
   genuinely-priceable remainder is recorded in the close block: `Semur` · `Sop Buntut` ·
   `Babi Guling` · `Pempek` · `Sambal Goreng Kentang`. ⚠️ Note that **there is no pork anywhere in
   the World Kitchen Indonesia file**, which is a real shelf gap Babi Guling would fix if the
   country is ever revisited.
5. ⏱️ **THE LANE TARGET IS NOW A RANGE, NOT A NUMBER.** China 50 ✅ · Japan 50 ✅ · **Indonesia 42 ✅**
   · Thailand and Vietnam to close on the same test rather than on a quota. Expected lane total is
   therefore **roughly 220–230 rather than 240**, and that is a better outcome than 240 with a
   tail of dishes written to fill a counter.
6. ⚖️ **THIS DOES NOT RELAX THE WOW BAR — IT IS THE BAR BEING ENFORCED.** The Shelf-WOW law says
   remove and replace boring recipes, never disguise them. Writing four thin cards to reach 50 is
   the same failure wearing a different hat, and A1-as-a-quota was quietly pushing toward it.
