## 1. RECIPE QUALITY – "The Adventure Rule"
- NO bland recipes (e.g., plain white rice, basic coffee, plain toast)
- EVERY recipe must have a "WOW factor" – interesting trivia, technique, or cultural story
- The app is an adventure – each dish must teach or delight
- Quality over quantity ALWAYS

## 2. VARIATIONS – Natural, Not Forced
- Some recipes: 5 variations (if it's a national icon like Bobotie)
- Most recipes: 2-3 variations (if it makes sense)
- Some recipes: 1 variation (the classic)
- RARELY: 0 variations (if it's perfect as-is)
- NEVER force a variation that doesn't work
- Natural spread is the goal, not a fixed number

## 3. LEFTOVER SUGGESTIONS
- MUST be specific to THAT ingredient/dish
- NO copy-paste across recipes (e.g., crab → crab cakes, crab fried rice)
- 2 researched suggestions per recipe (not 4 generic ones)
- Quality over quantity – 1 great suggestion > 4 copy-pasted ones

## 4. GOES WELL WITH
- MUST be specific and researched
- NO generic suggestions (e.g., "rice", "bread")
- Example: "Jasmine rice with fresh coriander" not just "rice"
- Each pairing must be thoughtful and relevant

## 5. YOUTUBE LINKS (Premium Feature)
- Add timestamped YouTube links for complex techniques
- Format: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ID&t=Xs
- Link to EXACT moment (e.g., 7:15 into the video)
- Safe – linking is not a copyright issue
- Add links throughout the app over time (mammoth task, but worthwhile)

## 6. TIMER
- Ensure ⏱️ timer markup is included and works as a clickable alarm

## 7. PRICING
- Double-check all costs against PRICE_DB
- Verify expensive dishes (R149+) ingredient by ingredient

## 8. GLOBAL ACCESSIBILITY – "The South African to Swedish Rule"
- The app starts in SA but MUST be accessible to ALL countries
- If a dish is regional (e.g., Boerewors), add a brief description:
  "A South African farmer's sausage..."
- Assume the reader is from ANY country – explain local ingredients
- Do the same for ALL regional dishes (Swedish, Italian, etc.)

## 9. BATCH SIZE
- 3 recipes at a time is the new standard
- Prevents errors, blocks, and corruption
- Ensures quality control on each batch
- Exceptions: If you're on a roll and the code is clean, 5 is fine
