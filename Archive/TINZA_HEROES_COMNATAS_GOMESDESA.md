# ★ CARD 2 — BACALHAU COM NATAS (Salt Cod with Cream)

**Region:** Portugal · **Type:** main (fish) · **Serves:** 4

**Story:** The creamy, comforting side of the bacalhau family — flaked salt cod and fried potato folded through a nutmeg-scented béchamel enriched with cream (*natas*), baked golden under a cheese crust. Where à Brás is a soft egg tangle, *com natas* is Portugal's answer to a gratin: the one everyone's grandmother makes a little differently.

**Ingredients**  *(buy-name · weight + pack hint · prep in method)*
- Salted cod (bacalhau) — 500 g *(dried-salted; soaks ~35% — locale block)*
- Potatoes — 600 g
- Onions — 2 medium
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Butter — 50 g
- Cake flour — 50 g
- Milk — 500 ml
- Fresh cream (natas) — 200 ml
- Grated cheese (Gruyère or mature cheddar) — 50 g
- Nutmeg — 1 small pinch
- Olive oil — for frying
- Black pepper — to taste *(NO added salt: cod salts the dish — taste the sauce before adding any)*

**Method**
1. **Desalt the cod** (shared line). *(Or Salt Your Own Cod.)*
2. Poach or simmer the cod 8–10 min, drain, flake, discarding skin and bones.
3. Cut potatoes into small cubes and fry in olive oil until golden and just tender; drain on paper.
4. Soften the sliced onions and garlic gently in a little olive oil until sweet — not browned. Fold the flaked cod through and warm 2–3 min.
5. **Béchamel:** melt the butter, stir in the flour to a roux, cook 1–2 min; whisk in the warm milk gradually to a smooth, thick sauce. Off the heat, stir in the cream, nutmeg and pepper. Taste — only then a little salt if it truly needs it.
6. Fold the fried potato and cod-onion mixture through about half the sauce; spread in a baking dish.
7. Top with the remaining sauce; scatter the cheese.
8. Bake at 200 °C for 20–25 min until golden and bubbling. Rest a few minutes and serve.

**Variations**  *(authentic default 🏆 + honest riffs)*
- 🏆 **Classic** (above).
- **SA cod swap (honest):** home-salted hake or salted snoek — snoek oilier/stronger, a bolder *not identical* version.
- **Lighter (honest riff):** half-cream/half-milk sauce, less cheese.
- **Weeknight-quick (honest):** shop white-sauce base + a hot 2–3 h quick-desalt — faster, a touch saltier.

**Leftovers**  *(easy-first · 2–4 · authentic · SA-realistic)*
- *(easy)* Reheat gently in a low oven with a splash of milk so the cream doesn't split.
- *(easy)* Spread on toast, crumb or cheese the top, grill to bubbling — a cod rarebit.
- *(creative)* Warm cod dip: stir in parsley and lemon zest, bake, serve with toasted bread or crudités.
- *(creative · authentic)* It's already cod + potato — mash, fold in an egg, fry into **bolinhos** (mini pastéis de bacalhau).

# ★ CARD 3 — BACALHAU À GOMES DE SÁ (Porto Salt Cod, Potato & Egg Bake)

**Region:** Portugal · **Type:** main (fish) · **Serves:** 4

**Story:** From Porto, named after José Luís Gomes de Sá, a 19th-century cod merchant whose recipe became a national treasure. Its signature is a quiet one: after desalting, the cod is steeped in warm milk to tenderise and mellow it. Then it's baked with boiled potato, sweet onions and plenty of good olive oil, and finished with hard-boiled egg, black olives and parsley — rustic, generous, and pure Porto.

**Ingredients**
- Salted cod (bacalhau) — 500 g *(dried-salted; soaks ~35% — locale block)*
- Potatoes (waxy) — 700 g
- Onions — 2 large
- Garlic — 3 cloves
- Milk — 250 ml *(to steep the cod)*
- Olive oil — 120 ml *(generous — this is an olive-oil-rich dish)*
- Eggs — 3 *(hard-boiled, to garnish)*
- Black olives (Portuguese) — 12
- Fresh parsley — 1 small bunch
- Bay leaf — 1 *(optional, with the potato boil)*
- Black pepper — to taste *(NO added salt: the cod carries it)*

**Method**
1. **Desalt the cod** (shared line). *(Or Salt Your Own Cod.)*
2. Pour boiling water over the cod (or poach briefly), then flake into large pieces, discarding skin and bones.
3. **Steep the flaked cod in warm milk for 1–2 hours** — the signature Gomes de Sá step; it tenderises and mellows the fish. Drain and discard the milk.
4. Boil the potatoes whole in their skins (bay leaf optional) until just tender; peel and slice into 1 cm rounds.
5. Soften the thinly sliced onions and garlic gently in olive oil until sweet and golden — not browned.
6. Oil a baking dish and layer the potato rounds, the onions, and the milk-steeped cod; grind over pepper and pour on a generous amount of olive oil.
7. Bake at 190–200 °C for 20–25 min until hot and lightly golden.
8. Garnish with sliced hard-boiled egg, black olives and chopped parsley. Serve.

**Variations**  *(authentic default 🏆 + honest riffs)*
- 🏆 **Classic Porto** (with the milk steep).
- **SA cod swap (honest):** home-salted hake or salted snoek — bolder, not identical.
- **Skip-the-steep quick (honest):** omit the milk soak for speed — slightly firmer, less mellow.
- **Lighter olive oil (honest):** reduce the oil — less rich, less traditional.

**Leftovers**  *(easy-first · 2–4 · authentic · SA-realistic)*
- *(easy)* Reheat gently in a low oven with a drizzle of olive oil.
- *(easy)* Fork warm or cold into a bowl with extra olives and a squeeze of lemon — a quick cod-and-potato salad.
- *(creative)* Crush potato and cod together, fold in an egg, and pan-fry into cakes — pastéis-style.
- *(creative · authentic)* Fold into beaten eggs and set as a Portuguese **tortilha**, olives running through it.
