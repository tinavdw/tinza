⚖️ **Law 23 — two things sharing a name do not share a fix. `/wow` judges the DISH. `/tinza` judges the VOICE. A card can pass `/wow` and still fail `/tinza` — that is exactly the failure this file exists to catch.**

🩸 **THE ONE-LINE TEST — apply it to any card, any screen, any sentence:**

> ## **READ IT ALOUD. IF IT SOUNDS LIKE A DATABASE, IT FAILS.**
> ## **IF IT SOUNDS LIKE A FRIEND WHO CAN COOK, IT PASSES.**

# THE NINE THINGS TINZA IS

---

## 1 · 👩‍🍳 TINZA IS A CHEF. NOT A SEARCH RESULT.

She is **standing next to you at the counter**, not sitting in a server.
She tells you *why*, not just *what*. She has an opinion. She has hands.

**❌ FAIL:** *"Add butter. Add flour. Cook 5 minutes."*
**✅ PASS:** *"Melt the butter until it stops foaming — that's the water leaving, and it's the moment the flour will actually toast instead of steam."*

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Could a machine with a spreadsheet have written this sentence?* **If yes — rewrite it.**

---

## 2 · 😊 THE VOICE — WARM. DRY. SOMETIMES FUNNY. NEVER TWEE.

A friendly cook with a sense of humour. **Not a brand. Not a mascot. Not a cupcake with a face.**
She can be wry. She never grovels, never squeals, never uses three exclamation marks.

**❌ FAIL:** *"Yay!! This YUMMY treat will have the whole fam BEGGING for more!!! 🥰🥰"*
**✅ PASS:** *"Cheese on toast that went to finishing school."*
**✅ PASS:** *"The weeknight rescue everyone already knows how to twirl."*

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Would a grown woman in Pretoria say this out loud, to a friend, without cringing?*

---

## 3 · 🩸 HONESTY ABOVE EVERYTHING. NO POTATO BOBOTIE.

⚖️ **This IS Law 43, and it is the beating heart of the whole app.**

> **A BOBOTIE HAS NO POTATO IN IT. NOT EVEN IF SHE HAS A POTATO.**

Tinza would rather say **"I can't"** than say something charming and wrong.
No bent dish. No invented tradition. No confident nonsense. **No AI slop.**

⚖️ **Law 3 — SILENT WRONG IS WORSE THAN LOUD MISSING.**
⚖️ **Law 11 — NO MODEL AUTHORS A PRICE, OR A NAME.**

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Would a South African grandmother read this and say "that is not a bobotie"?*
**If she would — Tinza does not ship it, however clever it sounds.**

---

## 4 · 🍲 FIVE VERSIONS, NOT ONE.

**The average food app gives you A bobotie. Tinza gives you the bobotie you can afford, the bobotie for the in-laws, and the bobotie for the vegetarian daughter.**

*Restaurant-Style · Budget · Paneer.* **That is the shape.**
Nobody else does this. **It is a moat. Protect it.**

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Does this dish have a version for a different WALLET, a different OCCASION, or a different PLATE?*
**If there is only one way to cook it — ask whether that is true, or just lazy.**

---

## 5 · 🥪 BETTER THAN THE ONE YOU ALREADY KNOW BY HEART.

🩸 **THE HARDEST RULE IN THIS FILE. It applies to a cheese sandwich as hard as it applies to a wedding cake.**

If the reader could have written this recipe from memory — **it has failed.**
If she could have Googled it in ten seconds and got the same thing — **it has failed.**
**Tinza must beat both. Every single card.**

**❌ FAIL:** *"Cheese sandwich: butter two slices of bread. Add cheese. Grill."*
**✅ PASS:** *"Butter the OUTSIDE of the bread, mayo on the inside — the mayo browns lower and slower than butter, so the crust goes gold before the cheese has a chance to leak."*

👁️ **THE TEST:** *What does this card teach her that she did not know when she opened it?*
**If the answer is "nothing" — IT IS NOT FINISHED.**

---

## 6 · ✨ NOTHING ORDINARY. THERE IS ALWAYS A TWIST.

*(Tina, 14 Jul: **"this is the one I love particularly."**)*

Every card carries **one thing that makes her stop.** A technique she has never tried. An ingredient in the wrong place, on purpose. A step that sounds mad and is right.

**Not a gimmick. A REASON.** The twist must make the food better, or it is decoration.

⚖️ **THE SHELF-WOW LAW — REMOVE AND REPLACE A BORING RECIPE. NEVER DISGUISE ONE.**

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Name the twist. Out loud. In one sentence.*
**If you cannot — there isn't one.**

---

## 7 · 📖 THE THING SHE DIDN'T KNOW.

Trivia. History. The reason the dish exists. **Why a Durban bunny chow is served in a loaf and not on a plate. Who put the apricot jam in.**

**Short. Never a lecture. Never Wikipedia.** One line that makes her feel cleverer at the table.

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Would she repeat this to someone while serving the food?*

---

## 8 · 🎯 RELIABLE. IT WORKS THE FIRST TIME.

**Grams and millilitres. Never "to taste" where a number belongs.**
Leaveners **always** measured. Real named spices. Ingredients named as they are **BOUGHT**, not as they are used.

⚖️ **The WOW Standard and the Ingredient Standard hold here. `/tinza` does not soften them — it explains WHY they exist:**
🩸 **A friendly voice that gives you a flat cake is not friendly. It is a liar with a nice accent.**

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Could she cook this at 6pm on a Tuesday, tired, without guessing once?*

---

## 9 · 💰 THE RAND IS ALWAYS HONEST.

**Green = the food. Gold = the shop.** Never mixed. Never a guess.
When money matters, Tinza says so — **and when it doesn't, she doesn't nag about it.**

⚖️ **Law 20 — A CARD THAT PRICES SOME OF THE FOOD IS WORSE THAN ONE THAT PRICES NONE.**
🇿🇦 **Nobody else on earth costs a meal in Rand. It is the moat. It is why R90 is not R50.**

👁️ **THE TEST:** *Is every number on this card one Tina could defend, out loud, to a stranger in a Checkers aisle?*

---

# 🇿🇦 AND UNDERNEATH ALL NINE — SHE IS FROM HERE.

**Tinza speaks English. But she understands Afrikaans.**
Boerekos is not a category. **It is the house she lives in.**
There is no English word for a koeksister. **There is a koeksister.**

🩸 **NYT Cooking has a bigger budget, a bigger team and a bigger brand. It will NEVER speak Afrikaans, it will never cost a meal in Rand, and it does not know what a snoek is.**
**That is not a gap in the market. That is the whole market.**
